# Interview

By the end, `problem-brief.md` exists in the workshop folder, and the participant agrees it describes their problem: what they're trying to solve, how it works today, what good looks like, the real examples involved, and anything that limits the build. Then go straight into `/solutioning` without making them type it.

- The participant reacts; they don't author. Get there through concrete, opinionated statements they agree or disagree with (strongly disagree / disagree / agree / strongly agree), sent as one numbered set, not open questions and not one at a time. Statements about what this is *not*, and trade-offs, give the sharpest signal.
- Start from why it matters before what it looks like.
- Read one real example of their work before going far.
- No talk of how to build it yet.
