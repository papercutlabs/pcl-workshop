# Retro

By the end, what this cycle taught is saved where the next session will use it, and the participant has heard exactly what was saved. Facts, preferences, corrections and decisions go to memory. Anything they'll run again becomes a skill, made with skill-creator. The output is what got saved, not a retro document.

- You go first: say concretely what you got wrong or nearly got wrong. The participant reacts and adds what you missed.
- Save each learning in a form that helps beyond this one moment.
- A learning that comes up mid-session is saved on the spot; it doesn't wait for the end.
